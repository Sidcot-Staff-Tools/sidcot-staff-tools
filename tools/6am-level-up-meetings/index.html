Sixth form meetings
6AM and Level Up

Every meeting picks up where the last one left off.

Each tutor group has one file in the sixth form SharePoint folder. Open it, pick a student, review last time’s targets and set new ones, then share them on Teams or by email home.

      What the tool does
      
        
      The meeting log records 6AM and Level Up meetings for a whole tutor group in one file, so each meeting starts from the targets set last time. Pastoral leads can view every group together in the sixth form overview.
      
        
      Meeting typeWhoTargets
      
          
      6AMEvery sixth form student, in routine tutor meetingsUp to three
      
          
      Level UpPriority students needing extended intervention, weeklyThree each week
      
        
      
      
      
      
      Before you start
      
        
        
          
      1. Use Chrome or Edge. Open the tool file by double-clicking it.
        2. 
          
      2. Add the sixth form SharePoint folder to your computer. Open the folder in SharePoint or Teams, click Add shortcut to My files, and it appears under OneDrive in File Explorer. Tutor group files are saved there.
        3. 
          
      3. Set up sharing once per computer. Click  and add the pastoral leads’ email addresses for Teams, plus your name and role for signing emails home.
        4. 
        
      
      
      
      
      Setting up your tutor group
      
        
        
          
      1. Click New tutor group on the home screen.
        2. 
          
      2. Enter the group name (for example 12AB), your name and the year group, then paste the students’ names, one per line.
        3. 
          
      3. Click Create and save and choose the sixth form SharePoint folder. The file is named after the group, for example 12AB.json.
        4. 
        
      
        
      Your students are listed down the left. Click a name to switch to that student. Use Add students for anyone who joins, and Mark as left the group in a student’s details for anyone who leaves; their records are kept.
      
        
      If you used the earlier one-file-per-student version, click Import student files and select those files to bring them into the group.
      
      
      
      
      Running a meeting
      
        
        
          
      1. Click the student’s name on the left.
        2. 
          
      2. Choose 6AM or Level Up. Students marked as Level Up students start with Level Up.
        3. 
          
      3. Review last time’s targets. Click Achieved, Partly or Not yet for each. Carry forward copies a target into this meeting’s targets.
        4. 
          
      4. Write Target 1, 2 and 3. Level Up sets three each week; 6AM can use fewer.
        5. 
          
      5. Add notes if applicable. Notes can be shared, so keep them suitable for the student and family.
        6. 
          
      6. Optional: open Staff-only notes and next meeting date.
        7. 
        
      
        
      A good target names the action, the evidence and when it’s due. Instead of “revise Chemistry more”, write “complete and self-mark two 30-minute organic chemistry question sets before Friday, and bring corrections to the next meeting”.
      
      
      
      
      Example targets
      
        
      If a student is stuck, click Choose an example under a target box, or Browse example targets. Pick an area or search, click a target, then type over each [bracketed] part. The share panel warns you if any [brackets] are left. Add targets you use often under Add your own example.
      
        
      Give students the Setting your targets guide before their meetings. It has the same examples, so they arrive with ideas ready.
      
      
      
      
      Saving
      
        
        
          
      • Click Save or press Ctrl+S. With Autosave ticked, changes save every few seconds.
        • 
          
      • The top bar shows Unsaved changes, Saving… or Saved with the time.
        • 
          
      • If the browser asks whether the page can edit the file, click Allow.
        • 
          
      • Working with a colleague. If someone else saves the same group file while you have it open (for example a co-tutor), the tool combines their changes with yours when you save, so neither of you loses work.
        • 
        
      
      
      
      
      Sharing on Teams and by email home
      
        
      The share panel writes both messages as you type. Nothing is sent until you press Send yourself. Staff-only notes are never included.
      
        
        
          
      • Open in Teams starts a chat with the pastoral leads, with the message typed. Copy message lets you paste it anywhere.
        • 
          
      • Open in email starts an email to the parent/carer. Tick Include notes only if they’re suitable for home. If a student has more than one parent or carer, put all their email addresses in student details, separated by commas, and the email goes to all of them. Check the addresses before sending.
        • 
        
      
      
      
      
      The next meeting
      
        
      Open your tutor group from Recent tutor groups on the home screen, or with Open a tutor group. Click a student: a new meeting is ready with last time’s targets listed for review. The student list shows when you last met each student, highlighted if it’s been more than three weeks.
      
        
      The bar chart has one bar per meeting: teal for 6AM, gold for Level Up. Taller means more targets achieved; a dashed outline means not yet reviewed.
      
      
      
      
      Printing and corrections
      
        
      Click Print, choose the student summary, the staff record or the complete history, then Print or save as PDF. To correct a meeting, click Open beside it in the meetings list, change it and save. Delete is only for meetings recorded by mistake.
      
      
      
      
      Sixth form overview (pastoral leads)
      
        
        
          
      1. On the home screen, click Open the sixth form folder and choose the SharePoint folder that holds the tutor group files. Next time, use the button with the folder’s name.
        2. 
          
      2. The overview reads every group file without changing anything. Filter by year or tutor group.
        3. 
          
      3. The tabs list Level Up students, students not met recently, students struggling with targets (under half achieved at two reviews running) and all students.
        4. 
          
      4. Click a student to see their full history, and print it if needed. Print summary prints the whole overview.
        5. 
        
      
        
      Click Refresh to pick up meetings tutors have saved since you opened the folder.
      
      
      
      
      Data protection and safeguarding
      
        
        
          
      • Keep tutor group files only in the sixth form SharePoint folder.
        • 
          
      • Never email or attach a group file. Share targets with the Teams message, the email home or a printed summary.
        • 
          
      • Write shared notes as if the student might read them.
        • 
          
      • Safeguarding concerns don’t belong in this tool. Report them through the school’s safeguarding system straight away.
        • 
        
      
      
      
      
      Troubleshooting
      
        
      ProblemWhat to do
      
          
      The SharePoint folder isn’t in the save windowAdd it with Add shortcut to My files in SharePoint, then look under OneDrive in the save window.
      
          
      “Click Save to allow saving”Click Save and choose Allow.
      
          
      A group is missing from Recent tutor groupsRecent groups are kept per computer. Use Open a tutor group.
      
          
      The overview doesn’t show a recent meetingThe tutor may not have saved yet, or OneDrive is still syncing. Wait a minute and click Refresh.
      
          
      Open in Teams is greyed outAdd email addresses in Sharing settings, and write at least one target.
      
          
      Open in email does nothingUse Copy email and paste into Outlook.
